What is a Ghost App?

A Ghost App is a real website given a permanent home on your computer. The installer creates a dedicated, sandboxed browser profile so the editor never touches your personal browsing data, never conflicts with extensions, and works completely offline after the first launch. It appears in your taskbar, can be pinned to your dock, and feels like a purpose‑built application — because it is. No Electron wrapper, no heavy downloads, just your existing browser running in a clean, isolated environment.
