About Forge™ (The Brand)

Welcome to Forge™: A philanthropic brand with a collection of 15+ open-source, client-side browser tools built on the God-led principle that creatives should own and control their software. We challenge the SaaS model which often leads to digital dispossession—where your access to your own work can be revoked by a price hike or a policy change.

Truly offline-first, our Community Edition tools are distributed as single, self-contained HTML files. No servers. No tracking. You download the tool, you own the code, and it is yours to command forever.

Tagline: Forge™. Own. Forever.

The Tool: WASM Video Editor

A professional-grade, 100% client-side Non-Linear Editor (NLE) running entirely in your browser. Powered by WebAssembly (FFmpeg.wasm), the HTML5 Canvas API, and the Web Audio API, this editor delivers a desktop-like workflow with zero server uploads, ensuring total privacy and instantaneous performance.

It features real-time Canvas compositing, a multi-track timeline, and a highly modular, hot-pluggable .js extension ecosystem.
